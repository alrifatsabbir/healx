Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know about finding doctors and booking care through HealiiX.

How does HealiiX verify doctor credentials?

Every doctor on HealiiX must provide verified Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council (BMDC) registration, institutional affiliations, and chamber credentials before being listed.

Can I search for doctors in specific cities?

Yes. Enter your city or district in the "Where?" search field alongside any specialty to view local chambers and visiting times.

Are online video consultations available?

Yes. Many specialists on HealiiX provide both physical chamber visits and high-definition video consultations for follow-ups and remote care.

Is there any additional booking fee?

No. HealiiX is free for patients to search and schedule consultations. You pay the standard doctor's consultation fee directly at the chamber or during online checkout.
