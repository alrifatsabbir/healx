1 Overview & Scope

HealiiX Health Inc. (“HealiiX”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) provides an integrated healthcare directory, doctor verification network, and verified appointment scheduling ecosystem across Bangladesh. We recognize that medical information is extraordinarily personal and sensitive.

This Privacy Policy describes the specific policies and safeguards governing the collection, utilization, retention, and protection of personal identifying information (PII) and protected health information (PHI) when using healiix.vercel.app and our associated mobile or web interfaces.

2 Information We Collect

A. Patient Information

  • Identity & Account Details: Full legal name, preferred username, email address, contact phone number, and birth date.
  • Optional Clinical Vitals: Blood group, height, weight, baseline blood pressure, blood sugar indicators, allergies, and emergency contact details.
  • Booking Details: Selected doctor chamber, scheduled appointment date/time, chief symptom description, and visit history.

B. Medical Practitioner (Doctor) Credentials

  • Professional Verification: Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council (BMDC) registration number, medical degrees, residency/fellowship records, and specialty qualifications.
  • Credential Documents: Secure digital scans of government NID/Passport and BMDC practice certificates. (Stored under strict administrator-only access and never made public).
  • Chamber & Practice Logistics: Hospital affiliations, chamber addresses, consultation fee structures, visiting hours, and serial management preferences.

3 How Information Is Used

We process your data exclusively for legitimate clinical and operational healthcare objectives:

Appointment Confirmation

Transmitting booking details, serial tokens, and chamber slot notifications directly between patient and doctor.

Board Verification

Validating practitioner licenses against official medical regulatory databases to protect patients from malpractice.

Automated Alerts & SMS

Dispatching transactional email/SMS reminders for scheduled appointments, queue delays, or chamber cancellations.

Platform Integrity

Guarding against fraudulent profiles, unauthorized session hijacking, and brute-force access attempts.

4 Doctor-Patient Confidentiality

Any symptom summaries, medical notes, or personal health metrics submitted by a patient during an appointment booking are shared solely with the booked practitioner and their authorized chamber assistant for that specific consultation.

HealiiX administrative staff cannot view private consultation notes unless explicitly requested by the patient during an ongoing technical dispute or mandated by a lawful judicial order in Bangladesh.

5 Storage & Cloud Infrastructure

All databases are hosted on encrypted enterprise clusters with strict IP allowlisting and role-based access control. Medical credential documents and user avatars are automatically converted to compressed, sanitized WebP binaries and hosted on dedicated Cloudinary CDN storage with SSL encryption.

Passwords are cryptographic one-way hashes salted with bcrypt (10+ rounds). No plain-text passwords ever exist on our servers, memory, or log files.

6 AI Clinical Triage Privacy

The HealiiX Triage Bot utilizes an offline, rule-based algorithmic classifier for standard symptoms (such as common headaches, fever, and rashes) and escalates to advanced medical LLMs only when ambiguous clinical nuance requires specialty routing.

When escalated, your session query is sanitized: no personal identifiers, names, phone numbers, or account IDs are included in the AI prompt. Conversation histories are stored client-side in your browser's session and are not used to train public machine learning models.

7 Our Zero-Sale-of-Data Pledge

HealiiX has never sold, rented, or monetized patient or practitioner data, and will never do so.

We do not license your health inquiries to pharmaceutical companies, commercial data brokers, or advertising networks. Our platform exists solely to facilitate clinical care and appointments.

8 Your Rights & Account Deletion

Under Bangladesh digital commerce and privacy standards, you maintain complete sovereignty over your information:

  • Right to Review: You may inspect all personal metrics and appointment history at any time from your Patient Settings.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update profile vitals, phone numbers, or emergency contacts instantly.
  • Right to Deletion: You may submit an account erasure request by emailing our privacy desk. Upon verification, all non-accounting records will be permanently purged within 14 business days.
