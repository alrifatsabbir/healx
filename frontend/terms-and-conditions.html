2 Platform Role & Emergency Protocol

HealiiX is a healthcare information directory and appointment scheduling technology provider. HealiiX is NOT an emergency responder, hospital, or ambulance provider.

The platform facilitates verified appointment bookings between patients and independent licensed medical practitioners. The independent doctor—not HealiiX—is solely responsible for medical diagnoses, prescriptions, treatment regimens, and clinical advice.

3 Patient Responsibilities

When booking care or interacting on HealiiX, patients agree to:

  • Provide accurate, truthful personal contact info, age, and valid medical history to ensure clinical safety.
  • Honor scheduled chamber visits or notify the doctor’s chamber at least 2 hours prior to the time slot if cancellation is unavoidable.
  • Pay the doctor’s published consultation fee directly at the chamber counter according to the clinic's local billing procedures.
  • Treat chamber medical staff, nurses, and assistants with professional courtesy.

4 Doctor BMDC Licensing & Representations

Any medical practitioner who applies to register on HealiiX represents, warrants, and agrees that:

  • They hold an active, valid registration number from the Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council (BMDC) without suspension or revocation.
  • All academic degrees (MBBS, BDS, FCPS, MD, MS) and specialized training listed in their profile are fully authentic.
  • Their profile undergoes rigorous human administrative review and BMDC registry cross-checking before public activation.
  • They maintain professional liability and practice standards under Bangladesh medical laws.

5 Bookings, Serials & Chamber Visits

HealiiX issues verified digital appointment tokens with assigned serial numbers. Serial call times provided are estimated; emergency cases handled by doctors at the clinic may naturally affect exact entry times.

HealiiX does not impose hidden platform booking surcharges on standard patient appointment scheduling. Doctor fees are displayed transparently on every profile.

6 AI Clinical Triage Disclaimer

The bot does not render binding medical diagnoses, write drug prescriptions, or replace in-person clinical examinations. Always verify health conditions directly with a licensed physician.
