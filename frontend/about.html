Why We Built HealiiX

Bridging the gap between patients and quality medicine.

In many parts of Bangladesh, finding the right specialist often involves long queues, confusing schedules, and unverified credentials. Patients frequently travel across cities without knowing if the doctor is available.

HealiiX resolves this by organizing a verified directory of doctors, showing accurate hospital chambers, transparent consultation fees, real patient reviews, and instant time-slot confirmations.
