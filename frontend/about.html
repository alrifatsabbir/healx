WHY WE BUILT HEALIIX

Bridging the gap between patients and quality medicine.

In many parts of Bangladesh, finding the right physician has historically meant long waiting queues, confusing schedule boards, and uncertainty regarding qualifications or consultation fees.

Patients frequently travel hundreds of miles without knowing if their doctor will be available that evening. HealiiX resolves this by organizing a verified directory of doctors, showing accurate hospital chambers, transparent fees in BDT, and instant appointment booking with zero middlemen markups.
