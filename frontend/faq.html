Help & Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple, transparent answers about appointments, BMDC-verified credentials, chamber directions, and platform services.

What is HealiiX and how does the Hybrid Model work?

HealiiX is a Hybrid Healthcare Platform designed specifically for Bangladesh. It serves both as an automated online appointment booking system and as a verified open doctor directory.

Patients who want instant appointment scheduling can book visiting time slots on the website. Patients who prefer offline convenience can use the direct Call for Serial phone number and follow live Google Maps directions to the chamber.

Is HealiiX free for patients to use?

Yes, 100% free for patients. There are no registration fees or service charges to search doctors, check visiting hours, or submit appointment bookings. Patients only pay the doctor's standard consultation fee directly at the chamber.

Does HealiiX cover doctors across all districts of Bangladesh?

Yes. HealiiX covers verified practitioners across all 64 districts. You can search by your specific city or district (e.g. Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Bogura, Barishal, Khulna, etc.) on the Find Doctors page to locate specialists close to home.

How do I book an appointment online?

1. Open Find Doctors and search by specialty, doctor name, or city.

2. Click View Profile ↗ on your chosen specialist.

3. In the sidebar, select your chamber location, preferred date, and time slot.

4. Fill in patient name, phone number, and brief notes, then click Confirm Appointment.

Can I call the chamber directly to book a serial?

Yes. Every doctor profile provides a direct Call for Serial button and the official chamber phone number so you or family members can book over a direct phone call.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Sign in to your patient account and view My Appointments. You can cancel or reschedule any upcoming consultation before the scheduled visiting hour.

How does HealiiX verify doctor credentials?

Each registered doctor must provide an official Bangladesh Medical & Dental Council (BMDC) Registration Number, degree certificates (FCPS, MD, MS, etc.), and institutional identity.

HealiiX administrators cross-verify every profile against official medical records before activating the public BMDC Verified Specialist shield badge.

How can a doctor join and create a profile?

Click Join as Doctor, choose the Doctor account type, enter your BMDC number, specialty, and chamber locations. Our verification team reviews submissions within 12–24 hours.

Are doctor government ID and medical certificates public?

No. Government ID files and degree certificate images are strictly confidential. Only the verified BMDC registration number and educational qualifications appear on the public profile. Sensitive documents are only accessible to administrators.

How do Google Maps directions work for chambers?

Every chamber on a doctor's profile features an interactive live Google Map embed and a Directions App ↗ link that opens turnkey turn-by-turn navigation on your mobile device.

Can a doctor have multiple chambers with different schedules?

Yes. Doctors can configure multiple chambers across different hospitals or diagnostic centers, each with independent visiting days, hours, fees, and contact numbers.

Is patient medical information kept confidential?

Yes. Patient profiles and appointment records are protected by strict access control. Only the patient, the consulted physician, and authorized system administrators can access appointment details.

What should I do during a medical emergency?

HealiiX is designed for planned outpatient specialist visits. In case of an emergency, please immediately call 999 or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.
